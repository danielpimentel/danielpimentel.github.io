BMI567 — Medical Image Processing

Instructor: Daniel Pimentel-Alarcón.

Open to graduate and senior undergraduate students. Hands-on introduction to medical image analysis techniques — formating, segmentation, registration, quantifcation, and classifcation. Students should have prior exposure to basic statistics and coding.
