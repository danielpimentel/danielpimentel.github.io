Outreach at SOL

Committed to inclusion, our goal is to bring science to the general audience and motivate the next generation to pursue studies in STEAM fields.

El Zoominario

Seminar series to disseminate science among general audiences. All videos are recorded and posted on YouTube.

WI Science Festival

10 Days of events for all ages in 52 counties celebrating science, art, and technology.

Sci-prov

Science through comedy. Scientists present their work in short, digestible presentations followed by brief Q&A. Comedians use all this material for their improv scenes.

Art and Science

Connecting art and science with programs like the Data as Art Exhibition, the Wiley Gallery for Art in Science, Science to Street Art, and the Kohler Fellows.

K12

Committed to inclusion in science, we strive to inspire the next generation of students to pursue STEM fields by engaging directly with schools that serve underrepresented students, like Notre Dame School of Milwaukee, Madison East High School, and Osborne High School.

Public Radio

Sharing with the public what life as a scientist is really like through Wisconsin Public Radio (WORT).
