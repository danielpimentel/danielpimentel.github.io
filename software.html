SOL Software

Principal Component Trees | Learns the set of nested subspaces that best fits a dataset. Principal component trees and their persistent homology, AAAI.
GrassCaré | Visualize arrangements of subspaces in the Grassmannian. Visualizing Grassmannians via Poincaré embeddings, SN Computer Science.
Grassmannian Fusion | Completes a data matrix according to a union of subspaces structure. Fusion over the Grassmannian for high-rank matrix completion, IEEE ISIT.
Elastic Stars | Clusters data according to a union of subspaces structure. Group-sparse subspace clustering with elastic stars, IEEE ISIT.
Deep Union Completion | Completes data according to a union of subspaces embedding. Deep union completion, AAAI.
